Two clicks per tab

Labels belong to a tab, so a label you set on one tab never shows up on another.

  1. Click the toolbar icon

    Type a label such as DEV, STAGE, PROD or CLIENT-A.

  2. See it everywhere

    The tab title starts with your label and a colored badge floats on the page.

  3. Drag it out of the way

    Move the badge anywhere. Its position is remembered for that tab.

Small tool, fewer accidents

It's the browser version of a red sticker on the production server.

Survives reloads

The label stays through refreshes and navigation within the same tab.

Stays in its tab

Closing or duplicating the tab clears the label, so it never leaks to other pages.

Your color

Set a preferred badge color once from the icon's right-click menu.

Title prefix

Labels show in the tab strip, in the window switcher and in your history.

No setup

No account and no domain rules. Label the tab you're on.

Private

Nothing is synced or sent anywhere. Labels live in the browser session.

Questions

Can it label tabs automatically by URL?

Not yet. Automatic rules by domain are the most requested feature and are next on the list.

Does it work in every frame?

The badge shows in the top-level page and stays above the site's own UI.

Why does it need access to all sites?

So you can label a tab on any domain you choose. It doesn't read or send page content.
