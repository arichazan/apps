Learn from the parts you missed

Podcasts are great input until you miss a sentence. EchoLingo shows you exactly what you missed.

  1. Find a show

    Search the podcast directory or paste any RSS feed. Subscribe like in any other player.

  2. Listen normally

    Background audio, lock-screen controls and adjustable speed and skip intervals.

  3. Tap when you're lost

    From the app, the notification or a headset button. You get the transcript and translation of the last few seconds.

  4. Keep the words

    Tap a word or phrase to save it. It goes into flashcards without duplicates.

Questions

Does it listen to my microphone?

Only during a request you start yourself. Podcast audio comes from the episode file, never from the mic.

Which languages?

Any language the speech and translation services support. Spanish, French, Portuguese, German and Italian first.

Will it cost money?

Expect a free tier with a few look-ups a day and a subscription for unlimited use.
