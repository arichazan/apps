Three moves, no new tab

Smartlate lives in the right-click menu, so you never copy text into a separate translator again.

  1. Select text

    Highlight a sentence, a paragraph or a whole email on any website.

  2. Right-click a language

    English rephrases in your chosen tone. Any other language translates and rephrases in one step.

  3. Paste it anywhere

    The result is already on your clipboard. Bold, links, lists and tables survive the paste into Gmail, Docs, Word or Slack.

Built for people who write across languages

It handles the polishing you would otherwise do by hand after a machine translation.

Your tone, every time

Pick professional, casual, concise or write your own prompt. Smartlate applies it to every translation.

As many languages as you need

Add Spanish, Hebrew, Japanese or any other language. Each one appears at the top level of the menu.

Formatting stays intact

Headings, bold, italics, links, lists and tables come back exactly as they went in.

Choose your AI

Works with OpenAI, Anthropic Claude or DeepSeek. Switch providers in settings at any time.

Your key stays on your device

API keys are kept in Chrome's local storage. Text goes only to the provider you chose.

Small and quick

No account, no dashboard and no sign-up. Install it, add a key and right-click.

Questions

Do I need my own API key?

Yes. You paste a key from OpenAI, Anthropic or DeepSeek once in settings. You pay that provider directly, usually cents a month.

Does Smartlate read my pages?

No. It only sees the text you select and send with a right-click.

Which languages work?

Any language your AI provider supports. You choose which ones appear in the menu.
