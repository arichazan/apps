From click to file in under a minute

Recording runs in a background document, so closing the popup never stops it.

  1. Pick your sources

    Toggle webcam, microphone and system audio. Choose your camera from the list.

  2. Choose what to share

    Chrome's own picker lets you record the whole screen, one window or a single tab.

  3. Stop and preview

    A preview page opens with the screen and webcam recordings side by side.

  4. Download

    Save MP4 files straight to your computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid tier, no watermark and no recording limit.

Where do my videos go?

To your Downloads folder. GrabScreen has no server and no cloud storage.

Can I record my face and screen together?

Yes. Turn on the webcam and it records next to the screen as a separate file, with a live preview while you record.
