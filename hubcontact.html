Add two keys, then just read your email

HubContact matches people by email address and phone number, including numbers it finds in the message text.

  1. Add your HubSpot key

    A read-only key for contacts, plus your portal ID so links open the right account.

  2. Add Stripe if you want

    A restricted read key shows plan, trial and cancellation status.

  3. Open a conversation

    The contact card appears under the subject line in Gmail or beside the chat in Bird Inbox.

Everything you'd otherwise check in another tab

Sales and support teams switch between their inbox, the CRM and Stripe dozens of times an hour. HubContact puts that information in one place.

Gmail and WhatsApp

Works in Gmail threads and in Bird (MessageBird) Inbox WhatsApp conversations.

Billing status at a glance

See whether someone is subscribed, trialing or about to cancel, and which plan they're on.

Click to call

Phone numbers become call links, in the card and in HubSpot's own phone fields.

Smart matching

Finds the customer in reply-to lines, form notifications, wa.me links and phone numbers in the text.

Stripe links in ActiveCampaign

Every email in your ActiveCampaign contact list gets a direct link to that customer in Stripe.

Read-only

HubContact only reads. It never edits your CRM or billing data.

Under the hood

Keys are stored in your browser and requests go directly to the HubSpot and Stripe APIs.

Questions

Do I need a HubSpot paid plan?

No. Any HubSpot account that can create a private app key with read access to contacts will work.

Is a one-click “Connect HubSpot” coming?

Yes. OAuth sign-in is next on the roadmap and will replace pasting keys.

Can it write notes back to HubSpot?

Not yet. Tell us if you need it.
