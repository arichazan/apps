Short daily games

Each round takes a couple of minutes, which is enough to keep your streak going.

  1. Letter Safari

    Flip-card flashcards for all 22 letters, with the sound, the final form and a silly mnemonic for each.

  2. Word Blitz

    Ten-question quizzes across greetings, food, animals, numbers and family. Correct answers in a row build combo multipliers.

  3. Memory Match

    Pair Hebrew words with their meanings. Fewer moves means a better score.

Questions

Is it free?

The first release is free to download.

Do I need to know the alphabet first?

No. Letter Safari starts with alef.

Why is there no sound?

Speech uses your phone's built-in Hebrew text-to-speech voice. Install one in Android settings if you don't hear anything.
