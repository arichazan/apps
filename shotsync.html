Pair once by QR code

A small receiver runs on your Mac and the Android app runs quietly in the background.

  1. Install the Mac receiver

    It starts at login, shows a menu bar icon and saves to the folder you choose.

  2. Scan the pairing QR

    Open ShotSync on Android and scan the code from the menu bar. Pairing codes expire after 5 minutes.

  3. Take a screenshot

    It appears in your Mac folder within about two seconds on the same Wi-Fi.

  4. Go offline, stay synced

    Away from home? Files wait in a queue and upload when your Mac is reachable again, including over Tailscale.

Fast and secure

It replaces emailing screenshots to yourself or waiting for Google Photos to sync.

No cloud in between

Phone to Mac, directly. No account, no third-party server and no upload limits.

Nothing gets lost

A persistent queue with retries, plus scans after reboots, makes sure every capture arrives.

Real security

HTTPS only, certificate pinning, revocable per-device tokens and SHA-256-verified writes.

Recordings too

Screen recordings sync the same way as screenshots.

Wi-Fi-only mode

Keep large recordings off your mobile data.

Open source

MIT licensed. Read the code and the protocol spec, or build it yourself.

Questions

Is there an iPhone version?

No. iPhones already have AirDrop. ShotSync is for Android phones with a Mac.

Does it work off my home network?

Yes, over Tailscale. Without a connection, files wait in the queue until your Mac is reachable.

Is it really free?

Yes, it's MIT-licensed open source.
