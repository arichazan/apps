EchoLingo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

EchoLingo ("the app") is a podcast player for language learners. It is operated by TR1.eu (Ari Chazan), Barcelona, Spain ("we"). Contact: support@itbcn.com. Public page: https://arichazan.github.io/apps/echolingo.html.

This policy covers the Android and iOS apps, the web app, and the EchoLingo clarification service (our server). We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. What leaves your device, and why

When you ask "What did they just say?" (a clarification)

  • A short podcast audio snippet (the last 5 to 15 seconds of the episode you are playing, as set under Settings → Look back; 8 seconds by default) and the episode's language code are sent to our server. Our server passes the snippet on to our transcription provider (OpenAI) to turn it into text. The snippet is cut from the podcast file that the app downloaded. It is never recorded from your microphone.
  • In the web app, instead of a snippet, the app sends the public episode audio URL and the start/end time. Our server downloads that public file, cuts out the same short window, and sends only that window to OpenAI.
  • Translations: when you pick a word or phrase, the selected text, the surrounding transcript (at most 2,000 characters) and the two language codes are sent to our server and on to OpenAI to produce a contextual translation.

Access token and install id

So that the free daily allowance can be counted, the app creates a random install id (not derived from any hardware, advertising or account identifier). It sends that id to our server once and gets back an access token. Both are stored in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and the token is sent with each clarification. Reinstalling the app creates a new id.

Other services the app contacts directly

  • Apple's podcast directory (itunes.apple.com) receives your podcast search terms.
  • Podcast publishers and their hosting/CDN providers receive the ordinary requests for RSS feeds, artwork and episode audio, as in any podcast player. They see your IP address under their own policies.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co) serves the optional offline transcription model, and GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) serves the optional offline dictionaries. These are downloaded only when you choose them in Settings → Offline mode.
  • Voice selection (optional, off by default): when you turn it on and start voice selection during a clarification, the microphone is active for at most about 7 seconds while you say the word you want. Recognition is done by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS), which may process that audio on its servers under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your voice audio.
  • Read aloud uses your device's built-in text-to-speech.

2. What we retain, exactly

Data Where How long
Podcast audio snippet Server memory; temporary file while cutting (web) Only while the request runs (seconds). Deleted when the request ends. Never written to logs or a database.
Downloaded episode file (web app only) Server memory and a temporary directory Deleted when the request ends
Transcript text, selected words, translations Passed through server memory Not stored by us. Returned to your app only.
Install id, daily clarification count Server database Count is kept for the current UTC day and deleted after it. The install id stays in the database while a count or entitlement exists for it.
"Unlimited" entitlement flag (subscribers, testers) Server database Until the subscription ends or you ask us to delete it
Request log line: time, endpoint, HTTP status, duration, byte or character count, pseudonymous identity (dev:<install id> or a one-way fingerprint of a tester token), request id Server logs at our host (Fly.io) Up to 30 days. No audio, transcript text, translations or IP addresses are written to our logs.
IP address Server memory, for rate limiting At most 1 hour. Our hosting provider's network edge also processes it.
Your library, playback positions, vocabulary, settings, cached episodes On your device only Until you delete them or uninstall the app

OpenAI receives the audio snippets and text described above as our processor. Under OpenAI's API data-usage policy, API data is not used to train models and may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse and misuse monitoring, then deleted. OpenAI may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on OpenAI's Data Processing Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Fly.io (our host, servers in Madrid, Spain) processes requests and stores the logs and database described above as our processor.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Providing the features you ask for (clarification, translation, free allowance): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Rate limiting, abuse prevention and service logs: legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and available (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Purchases

If paid plans are offered, payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details. We receive only an anonymous app-user id and whether your subscription is active, to switch the unlimited entitlement on or off.

5. Children

EchoLingo is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Spain) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. Email support@itbcn.com and include your install id if you can, so we can find your daily counter or entitlement. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. You may also complain to the Spanish data-protection authority (AEPD, https://www.aepd.es).

7. Security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. Your access token is kept in the operating system's secure storage. Server secrets are stored as platform secrets, never in the app.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when practices change. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
