TwoTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

TwoTrack is a macOS menu-bar app that records your Mac's audio output and, optionally, your microphone. This policy explains what the app does with that audio and with any other information. Short version: your recordings stay on your Mac. TwoTrack has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Operator: TR1.eu (Ari Chazan), Barcelona, Spain Contact: support@itbcn.com Product page: https://arichazan.github.io/apps/twotrack.html

1. What TwoTrack records, and when

  • Mac audio (system audio): the sound your Mac is playing, captured with Apple's ScreenCaptureKit under the macOS "Screen & System Audio Recording" permission. TwoTrack never captures, stores or transmits screen images or video. macOS requires that permission even for audio-only capture.
  • Microphone (optional): the microphone you pick, under the macOS "Microphone" permission. You can turn "Include microphone" off, in which case the microphone is never opened and the permission is not needed.
  • Monitoring vs. recording: once set up, TwoTrack listens to both sources to drive the level meters (macOS shows its orange recording indicator while it does). That audio is analysed in memory for the meter and discarded immediately. Nothing is written to disk until you press Record, and writing stops when you press Stop, when the Mac goes to sleep, or when a permission is withdrawn.

2. Where recordings are stored

Each recording is a folder on your Mac (default ~/Movies/TwoTrack/<title>/) with System Audio.m4a, Microphone.m4a, Meeting Mix.m4a and metadata.json. While a recording is in progress, temporary "scratch" files (*.traw) in the same folder let TwoTrack rebuild the recording after a crash. They are deleted when the recording finishes normally, or after recovery.

metadata.json contains the title, start/end times, duration, pause times, the microphone's device name, peak levels, the app version and the recording's status. It contains no speech content or transcript.

Recordings are ordinary files: you can move, back up or delete them with the Finder. If your folder is synced by a service you chose (e.g. iCloud Drive, Dropbox), that service's terms apply to the copy it makes — TwoTrack does not upload anything itself.

3. What TwoTrack does not do

  • No network requests for recording, playback or setup. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud storage, no transcription service, no AI processing of your audio.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with anyone.

If a future version adds an optional network feature (for example licence activation or update checks — see below), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and audio, titles and file names will never be sent.

4. Settings and logs on your Mac

  • Preferences (setup completed, consent reminder on/off, storage folder, chosen microphone, include-microphone) are stored in the standard macOS preferences for TwoTrack on your Mac.
  • TwoTrack writes diagnostic messages to the macOS unified log on your Mac (e.g. "recording started", device names, error codes). Recording titles are marked private and redacted by macOS. These logs leave your Mac only if you choose to share them, for example in a support request or an Apple sysdiagnose.

5. Purchases, licence keys and updates

TwoTrack is sold directly. Payment is handled by the reseller / merchant of record you buy from (for example Lemon Squeezy, Paddle or Gumroad), under its own privacy policy; we receive the order details that reseller shares with sellers (such as your name, email address, country and order number) to provide your licence and support. We never see your full card details.

Versions that check a licence key or look for updates contact only the licence provider's or our update server, sending the licence key, a random installation identifier, the app version and the macOS version — never audio, titles or file names. Until such a version ships, the app makes no network connections.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You control every recording and can delete it at any time in the Finder.
  • You can revoke Microphone or Screen & System Audio Recording access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Uninstalling: quit TwoTrack, delete the app, and optionally delete ~/Movies/TwoTrack and ~/Library/Preferences/com.twotrack.TwoTrack.plist.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over the purchase data we hold, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es). Email support@itbcn.com to exercise them.

7. Recording other people

Recordings may contain other people's voices. You are responsible for obtaining any consent required by law or by your organisation before recording. See CONSENT.md.

8. Children

TwoTrack is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on the product page and in this file, with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes (for example, a new network feature) will be described in the release notes of the version that introduces them.
