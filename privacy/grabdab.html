GrabDab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

GrabDab is a macOS menu-bar app that turns a screen area you select into text, or reads the QR code or barcode in it, and copies the result to your clipboard.

Operator: TR1.eu (Ari Chazan), Barcelona, Spain Contact: support@itbcn.com Web: https://arichazan.github.io/apps/grabdab.html

Summary

GrabDab does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal data. It makes no network connections. All recognition happens on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework.

What the app accesses and why

Access Why Where it goes
Screen Recording permission (macOS) To capture only the rectangle you drag after pressing the hotkey or choosing Capture from the menu bar The captured image is held in memory just long enough to recognize it. It is never written to disk or sent anywhere.
Clipboard To place the recognized text or code payload on the clipboard Stays on your Mac, managed by macOS
Preferences (hotkey, language, toast, launch at login) To remember your settings Stored locally in the app's sandboxed preferences file

GrabDab keeps the most recent capture in memory only while the app runs, so the same image can be re-recognized. It is discarded when the app quits.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs
  • No network access: the Mac App Store build is sandboxed without the network entitlement
  • No data sharing with third parties

Optional Tesseract build (direct download only)

A direct-download build may be compiled with an optional fallback that runs a Tesseract OCR program you installed yourself. It runs locally, gets a temporary image file that is deleted straight after, and makes no network connections. The Mac App Store version does not include it.

Children

GrabDab collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can revoke Screen Recording access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording. Uninstalling the app removes its preferences container.

Data protection rights (GDPR)

Because the operator processes no personal data through the app, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you write to support, your email is used only to reply to you and is not shared.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the address above with a new date.
