Privacy Policy

Product: the screen and webcam recorder Chrome extension published from this repository (working name "GrabScreen"; the store name is set in RecordScreenExtension/_locales/en/messages.json). Operator: TR1.eu (Ari Chazan), Barcelona, Spain Contact: support@itbcn.com Website: https://arichazan.github.io/apps/grabscreen.html Last updated: 2026-10-31

Summary

The extension records your screen, and optionally your webcam, microphone and system/tab audio, only on your computer. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. It makes no network requests. Nothing you record or configure is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the extension handles, and where it stays

Data Why Where it is kept How long
Screen, window or tab video and audio you choose in Chrome's picker To make the recording you started In memory while recording, then in the extension's local IndexedDB storage on your disk From the moment you press Stop until you have saved every recording from the preview page, or pressed Discard. If you close the preview without doing either, it stays until you start your next recording (which clears it) or remove the extension.
Webcam video and microphone audio (only if you turn them on) Webcam recording, live camera preview, narration Same as above Same as above
Your settings: webcam/system audio/microphone on or off, and the ID of the camera you picked So the popup remembers your choices chrome.storage.local on your computer Until you change them or remove the extension
Recording status, start time and the last error message To show the timer and errors in the popup and name the file chrome.storage.local / chrome.storage.session on your computer Cleared when the recording is saved or discarded, or once the error has been shown. Session storage is cleared when Chrome closes.
Finished MP4 or WebM files The output you asked for Your Downloads folder (or wherever Chrome saves downloads) Until you delete them. They are your files.

Keeping the unsaved recording until it is saved means that a crash, or reloading the preview tab, does not lose it. It never leaves your computer.

The MP4 conversion runs in your browser using Chrome's built-in video encoders. No conversion service is used.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, recordings or usage data.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, trackers or ads.
  • No remotely hosted code. All code ships inside the extension package.
  • We do not read your browsing history or the content of web pages. The extension only sees what you choose to share in Chrome's screen picker, and only while you are recording.

Permissions

  • storage: keep your settings and the recording status on your computer.
  • unlimitedStorage: lets long recordings be held in local storage between Stop and the preview page without hitting Chrome's default quota.
  • offscreen: Manifest V3 requires recording to run in an offscreen document, which lets the recording continue after the popup closes.

Chrome asks you directly before the extension can capture the screen (every time), the camera or the microphone. You can revoke camera and microphone access at any time in Chrome's site settings for the extension.

Children

The extension is a general-purpose tool. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
