Filternizer Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 October 2026

Filternizer ("the app") is operated by TR1.eu (Ari Chazan), Barcelona, Spain ("we", "us"). Contact: support@itbcn.com. Homepage: https://arichazan.github.io/apps/filternizer.html

This policy covers the Filternizer Android app and the Filternizer Chrome extension. The optional macOS companion tools run on your own Mac under your own Google credentials (see section 7).

1. Summary

  • Filternizer reads and changes your Gmail only to do what you ask on screen: show your Inbox as cards, open a message, create a Gmail filter, archive, label, trash or reply.
  • Your Gmail data is processed on your device. It goes directly between your device and Google's Gmail API. We do not operate any server that receives your email, and we do not collect, store, sell or share it.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking.

2. Google user data we access, and why

When you connect Gmail, you grant two Google permissions:

Permission What Filternizer does with it
gmail.modify ("Read, compose, and send emails… and modify labels") Lists Inbox and @a_SafeDelete messages (sender, recipient, subject, date, read/unread state, Gmail's short snippet). Downloads a message body only when you open it. Applies the label, archive, Trash and undo actions you choose. Applies a new filter to existing matching mail. Sends a reply you write.
gmail.settings.basic ("Manage your basic mail settings") Creates the Gmail filter you confirm in the editor. Lists your already-verified forwarding addresses so you can pick one for a filter.

Filternizer never permanently deletes mail. Messages you remove go to Gmail Trash, where Gmail's normal 30-day recovery applies.

Filternizer also stores your Google account email address on the device, so it knows which account is connected.

3. How data is stored

  • On your device only, in the app's private storage: the email address of the connected account, a cache of up to 100 message cards per folder (sender, recipient, subject, snippet, date, ids), a queue of filters waiting for a network connection, your filter-editor defaults, and (if you connect Trello) your Trello API key, token and board choice.
  • This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • OAuth access tokens are managed by Google Play services (Android) or Chrome's identity API. Filternizer does not write them to its own storage.
  • Message bodies are kept in memory only while the reader is open.
  • The Chrome extension stores only your preferences (last label, skip-Inbox, forwarding choice) in Chrome storage. It keeps the current message id in session storage while the side panel is open. It does not store messages or tokens.

4. How data is shared

We do not sell, rent or transfer Google user data. Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Google: every Gmail operation is a request to Google's Gmail API, as described above.
  • Trello (only when you tap "Add to Trello"): Filternizer creates a card in your Trello account. The card contains the message subject, the sender's name and address, the recipient address, the received date and Gmail's short snippet. It goes directly to Trello (api.trello.com) with the Trello token you authorised. Trello's own privacy policy applies to that card.
  • AI Delegator (optional, off by default): this feature is available only in builds configured with your own Firebase project and paired with your own Mac. It is not configured in the published app. If it is enabled, job state (message id, subject, sender, the task proposal and your approvals) passes through that Firebase project to your Mac. We do not operate that project.
  • Legal: we would disclose data only if required by law. We hold no Gmail data to disclose.

No human at TR1.eu reads your email. The only exception would be if you explicitly send us a message for support and ask us to look at it.

5. Limited Use disclosure

Filternizer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We use Gmail data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described in section 2, which you can see in the app.
  • We transfer Gmail data to others only as described in section 4: to provide those features at your request, for security purposes, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use Gmail data to serve advertisements, including personalised or retargeted ads.
  • We do not allow humans to read Gmail data unless you give us affirmative consent for specific messages, it is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), it is needed to comply with law, or the data is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.
  • We do not use Gmail data to train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models, and we do not transfer it to third parties for that purpose.
  • We do not sell Gmail data, and we do not use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect: sign out in the app, or remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions → Filternizer → Remove access. Revoking stops all Gmail access immediately.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app (Android), remove the extension (Chrome), or use Android Settings → Apps → Filternizer → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Filters you created are ordinary Gmail filters. They stay in your Gmail account until you delete them in Gmail → Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses. The @a_SafeDelete label and its messages also stay in Gmail.
  • Trello: disconnect in the app's Trello settings. This revokes the token. Delete cards in Trello.
  • We hold no account or email data on our servers, so there is nothing else to delete. You can still email support@itbcn.com with any request and we will confirm within 30 days.

7. macOS companion (optional)

The ai-delegator and filter-cleaner command-line tools run on your Mac using an OAuth token you create in your own Google Cloud project. They store their state locally in ~/Library/Application Support/Filerizer/. filter-cleaner contacts unsubscribe URLs or addresses taken from your own mail, and only when you approve each one. ai-delegator runs a locally installed coding agent on messages you label AIDelegate, after you approve it on your phone. That agent's own terms and data practices apply to whatever you approve it to process.

8. Children

Filternizer is not directed to children under 16 and does not knowingly process their data.

9. Security

All network traffic uses TLS. Filternizer requests the narrowest Gmail permissions that support its features (it does not request full IMAP/https://mail.google.com/ access). Report security issues to support@itbcn.com.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

The operator is established in Spain. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Because we store no personal data on our systems, most requests are fulfilled by the steps in section 6. You may complain to the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (https://www.aepd.es).

11. Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. If a change affects how Google user data is used, we will ask for your consent in the app before it applies.
