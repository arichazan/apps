AlefBet! Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 App: AlefBet! (Android, package eu.tr1.alefbet) Operator: TR1.eu (Ari Chazan), Barcelona, Spain Contact: support@itbcn.com Web: https://arichazan.github.io/apps/alefbet.html

Summary

AlefBet! does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and the app does not use the advertising ID. Your learning progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following locally, in its private app storage on your phone:

  • Learning progress: XP, level, daily streak, the date you last practised, and best scores for each game.
  • Settings: the course you picked and the interface language you chose.
  • A cached copy of the public vocabulary file (see below), so the app works offline.

This data is never sent to us. You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the app or using Settings → Apps → AlefBet! → Storage → Clear data.

If you have Android's system backup turned on, Android may include this progress in your own device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings. We cannot access it.

Network use

The app makes one kind of network request. It downloads a public vocabulary file (vocab_manifest.json) over HTTPS from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com/arichazan/alefbet-content). This is how new words and courses arrive without an app update.

  • The request carries no account, identifier, progress or other personal data. It is a plain file download.
  • Like any website, GitHub's servers receive the technical data that every internet request carries, such as your IP address and a basic user-agent string. GitHub processes it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive this data and have no access to it.
  • The app refuses unencrypted (HTTP) connections.

If you are offline, the app uses its cached or built-in content and works normally.

Text-to-speech

AlefBet! reads words aloud using the text-to-speech (TTS) engine already installed on your device, such as Google's Speech Services. The app passes only the word being spoken to that engine and receives no data back. If a Hebrew voice is missing, the app can open the engine's voice-download screen or its Google Play page. Those are separate apps with their own privacy policies.

Children

AlefBet! collects no personal information from anyone, children included. It has no ads, chat, user-generated content, social features or in-app purchases. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side. Clearing the app's data on the device (see above) removes all progress.

Permissions

  • Internet is used only to download the public vocabulary file described above.

The app requests no other permissions. It has no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or the advertising ID.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you have a question or concern, write to support@itbcn.com. If you are in the EU, you can also contact your local data-protection authority. In Spain, that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Changes

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change ships.
