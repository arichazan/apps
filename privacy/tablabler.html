TabLabler Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 October 2026

TabLabler is a Chrome extension operated by TR1.eu (Ari Chazan), Barcelona, Spain. Contact: support@itbcn.com

Summary

TabLabler does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. Everything it stores stays in your browser on your device. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking or ads.

What is stored, and where

Data Where How long
The label, color and badge position you set for a tab chrome.storage.session (in memory) Until the tab is closed or the browser quits
Your URL rules (pattern, label, color) chrome.storage.local on this device Until you delete the rule or remove the extension
Your preferred badge color chrome.storage.local on this device Until you reset it or remove the extension

Nothing is stored in chrome.storage.sync, so nothing is synced through your Google account. (Versions before 1.0 kept the preferred color in sync storage; 1.0 moves it to local storage and deletes the synced copy.)

Page access

TabLabler starts with no access to websites. It changes a page only:

  • when you click its toolbar icon on that tab (Chrome's temporary activeTab access), or
  • on sites you granted for a URL rule, or for all sites if you turn that option on.

On those pages it adds a badge element and puts your label at the start of the page title. To match rules it compares the page address with your patterns inside the browser. It does not read, record or send page content, form data, cookies or browsing history.

Third parties

None. TabLabler includes no third-party code, services or SDKs.

Children

TabLabler is a general-purpose developer tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this file and on https://arichazan.github.io/apps/tablabler.html with a new effective date.
