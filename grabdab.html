On-screen text grabber

If you can see it, you can copy it.

Press ⌘⇧2 and drag over any text on your screen: a screenshot, a video frame, a locked PDF or an error dialog. GrabDab reads it on your Mac and puts it on your clipboard.

One shortcut

GrabDab lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon or window to manage.

  1. Press ⌘⇧2

    A light overlay covers the screen. Change the shortcut in Preferences.

  2. Drag over the text

    Select any area. Press Escape or right-click to cancel.

  3. Paste

    The text is already on your clipboard, with line breaks kept.

Fast and private

Recognition runs on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework, typically in under two seconds.

Works on anything visible

Images, videos, screen shares, scanned PDFs, apps that block copying.

QR codes and barcodes

Use the same gesture on a QR code or barcode to copy its contents.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No cloud OCR, no account and no network calls.

Keeps line breaks

Multi-line text, addresses and code snippets paste with their original layout.

Questions

Why does it need Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires that permission for any app that reads pixels on screen. GrabDab only captures the area you select.

Which languages are supported?

Ten: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese and Korean.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your Mac.
