Set it up once, then forget it

First-run setup takes about a minute. After that, recording is one click in the menu bar.

  1. Grant two permissions

    Microphone, plus Screen & System Audio Recording. TwoTrack checks both before your first call.

  2. Watch both meters move

    Speak and play some audio to confirm both sources are working before the meeting starts.

  3. Click Record

    Use any meeting app. Switch from Zoom to Meet halfway through and the recording keeps going.

  4. Open the folder

    You get your voice, the other side and a mix as separate files, ready for transcription.

Recordings you can rely on

Most Mac recorders depend on fragile audio routing. TwoTrack uses Apple's ScreenCaptureKit instead.

No virtual driver

No BlackHole and no fake speaker in your sound settings. Your normal audio setup stays as it is.

Separate tracks

Your mic and the remote side are saved as separate files, which makes transcription and editing much easier.

Crash-safe

If your Mac restarts mid-call, TwoTrack rebuilds the recording from its scratch files the next time it launches.

Disk-aware

Shows how many hours of space you have left and warns you before it runs out.

System audio only mode

Record just a webinar or lecture with the microphone off.

Local files

Recordings are saved in your Movies folder and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Does the other side know I'm recording?

TwoTrack doesn't notify anyone. Recording laws vary, so always tell participants and get their consent.

Does it work with headphones?

Yes. It captures the audio playing through your Mac, so headphones make no difference.

Does it transcribe?

Not yet. The separate tracks are made to work well with transcription tools like Whisper.
