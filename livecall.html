How a call works

Two independent translation streams run at once, one for each direction.

  1. Pick your languages

    The source language is detected automatically. You choose the target for each direction.

  2. Start both directions

    Your microphone goes out translated and the caller's voice comes back translated to your headphones.

  3. Call as usual

    Works with Zoiper and other softphones, plus WhatsApp desktop with a simple audio setup.

  4. Read along

    Original and translated subtitles scroll live, so you can catch anything you missed.

Questions

Does the other person need to install anything?

No. They hear a normal voice call in their own language.

How much latency is there?

Typically one to two seconds, a little slower than a human interpreter.

Is setup hard?

The current prototype needs two virtual audio devices. The native Mac app is being built to remove that step.
